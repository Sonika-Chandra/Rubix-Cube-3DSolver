How to play

  • Turn a layer: drag a sticker. The layer follows your finger and snaps to the nearest quarter turn.
  • Turn a middle layer: drag a sticker in the middle row or column, or press M, E, S (M turns like L, E like D, S like F).
  • Turn the whole cube: hold Shift and drag, or press X, Y, Z.
  • Look around: drag the empty space around the cube. Scroll to zoom. Arrow keys work too.
  • Keyboard: U R F D L B turn the face that's currently up, right, front…; M E S turn the middle slices. Hold Shift to turn the other way. Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Race it: press Scramble. Your timer starts on your first turn and stops when the cube is solved. Then compare: see how many moves each method needs for the same scramble, and press Watch to race it.
  • Stuck? 💡 Hint names the next step, ▶ Show me plays just that step, 🏁 Finish it solves the rest. Your timer keeps running and the result is marked "with help".
  • Watch it solve: choose a method and press Solve. The timer runs while it turns and stops the moment it's solved.

Notation: R = right face clockwise (as you look at it), R' = anticlockwise, R2 = half turn.
